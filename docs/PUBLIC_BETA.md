# Scope

JANMARG is a student app for five Ministry of Tribal Affairs schemes. It is not an official government portal.

Live in this build:

- Scheme notes, readiness, and JAGO answers from reviewed public notes
- Email sign-in, plus Google or X when the visitor is not already signed in
- A practice DigiLocker file. No Aadhaar number, bank page, or OTP

Not live:

- Submitting an application to NSP, DBT, or Canara SFMP
- A payment, a sanction, or an eligibility decision
